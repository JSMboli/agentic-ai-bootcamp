Programme outline

Indicative bootcamp flow

Day 1 · 18 Aug 2026

Foundations of Agentic AI

Introductions to LLMs, agents, workflows, prompts, tools, providers and the role of Human-in-the-Loop supervision.

LangGraph and multi-step orchestration

Students build structured graphs, connect tools and explore controlled versus model-driven workflows.

Human approval, capstone patterns and reflection

FAQ

Common student questions

Do I need a paid API key to take part?

No. The resources are designed to be financially inclusive. Students can use Groq, local Ollama models, or an offline fallback path where appropriate.

Which notebook should I open first?

Start with the Day 0 setup and provider diagnostics notebook. It is designed to confirm that your environment is working correctly before progressing further.

Why are progress indicators shown in the notebooks?

Some models and workflows take time to run. The revised notebooks include progress feedback, timing details and model metadata so you always know what is happening.

What should I pay attention to during the Amazon visit?

Observe automation, decision support, safety, process optimisation, human supervision, coordination and operational workflows that could benefit from agentic AI.
